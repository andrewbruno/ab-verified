What you are watching

  1. Staff review a job the client submitted, and set its bid closing date.
  2. Staff choose who may bid. Nobody else can see the job at all.
  3. The invited contractor accepts and lodges a bid.
  4. Staff screen the bids before the client ever sees them.
  5. The client compares the released bids and selects one.
  6. Staff confirm the award, which finally releases contact details.

Three people, not one workflow

The video signs in and out as Staff, Contractor and Client in turn, because no single account can perform this journey. That is the marketplace's design rather than a limitation of the demo: every meaningful transition needs a person on the other side of it.
