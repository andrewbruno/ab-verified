You have no invitations yet

AB-Verified is a curated marketplace, not a job board. Contractors do not browse open work and bid on whatever looks promising. Instead, Staff read each approved job and invite a small number of verified businesses whose skills, category and region fit the work.

That is why this page is empty rather than showing you a list to search. Nothing is hidden behind a filter you have not found: a job you have not been invited to genuinely does not exist for your account.

The best thing you can do is keep your capability profile current. Your skills, categories and region are what Staff match against, and an invitation arrives by email and SMS the moment one is issued.
