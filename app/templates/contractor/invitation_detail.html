Who else is bidding?

We do not disclose that. You cannot see the number or identity of the other contractors invited, and they cannot see yours. You are competing on your proposal, not on a visible count.

Client contact details

The client is a verified Australian business. Their contact details are released to you if you win the work, and yours to them at the same moment. Until then, please keep contact details out of your bid: we scan for them and flag them to Staff.
