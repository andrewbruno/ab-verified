Curated, not listed

Work is pushed to a short list of suitable contractors rather than pulled from an open board. Three things happen before a contractor ever sees a job, and a person does each of them.

Step one

Staff verify the business

Every registering business supplies an ABN. We check it against the Australian Business Register, compare the entity name with the name supplied, and read the supporting documents. A person makes the decision; the automation only assembles the evidence.

Step two

Staff approve the job

A posted job is reviewed before it goes anywhere: scope, budget and closing date. Contact details are held back until the work is awarded, so the marketplace is not a route around itself.

Step three

Staff invite the contractors

We choose the contractors the job actually suits and invite them. Nobody else can see it. A contractor who was not invited cannot find the job at all, because the database refuses the read.
