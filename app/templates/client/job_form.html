Before you submit

Our team reviews every job before any contractor is approached. We may come back to you if the scope or the budget needs tightening. Once a job is submitted you cannot edit it, so read it through first.

Who will see this

Only our team, and the contractors we invite. Jobs are never published publicly, never listed on an open board and never indexed by a search engine.

Contact details

Please keep email addresses and phone numbers out of the description. We hold contact details back until the work is awarded, which is what keeps bidding honest for everyone. If we spot any, we will flag the job rather than reject it out of hand.
