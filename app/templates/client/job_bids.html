What you can and cannot see here

You see the contractor's business name, region and capability. Email addresses, phone numbers and street addresses are held back until our team confirms the award, which is what stops either side stepping around the marketplace mid-bid.

Selecting a bid does not award the job. It moves the job to award pending so our team can confirm it. Only then does the winning contractor get the work, the other bids move to not selected, and contact details are released to both sides.
