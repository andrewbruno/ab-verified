The daily loop

  1. Verify the registrations that are waiting.
  2. Moderate the jobs, setting a bid closing date on approval.
  3. Curate and invite contractors for approved jobs.
  4. Screen bids, then release the ones the client should see.
  5. Confirm awards and release contact details.
